Interactive Image Map

With HTML image maps you can make clickable areas on an image.

After you put your image in your HTML...

You then, start with the 'map' tags...Inside the 'map' tags you put the 'area' tags...The area tag is where we tell the browser to look for the clickable areas.

In the area tag we specify the 'coords' or coordinates...the shape: rect, circle, poly or default...and where you want the clickable area to go w/a 'href' attribute.

You can find these 'coords' with a image editor like Microsoft Paint. You have to do each axis separately.

coord="x-axis1, x-axis2, y-axis1, y-axis2"
